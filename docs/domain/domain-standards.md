# Domain Standards — IT & AI

Alignment mapping for this core. *Aligned to* throughout: an AIMS (ISO/IEC 42001)
certification is an organizational program; this core supplies its technical evidence
surface — model registry, gates, budgets, drift and governance verdicts.

## Concept mapping

| Platform concept | Domain standard | Mapping |
|---|---|---|
| Model registry & lifecycle | ISO/IEC 42001 (AI system life cycle) / ISO/IEC 22989 terminology | Registered → approved → retired lifecycle |
| Deployment gates | ISO/IEC 23894 risk treatment; NIST AI RMF Govern/Map | Risk-tiered rules with reasoned decisions |
| Budgets & usage | FinOps-for-AI practice | Guarded quota accounts; the no-overrun invariant |
| Drift & quality | ISO/IEC 25059 quality posture | Drift scoring and governance evaluation functions |
| Service operation | ISO/IEC 20000 | Runbook/SLO discipline |

## Scope boundaries

This core does not train models, store prompts, or judge content. It governs the
platform around models: identity, lifecycle, budgets, gates and evidence.
